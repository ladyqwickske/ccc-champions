Penalty Exemptions

Members ticked here never receive an event penalty and are left out of the period-end underperformer report (crypting norm). Use this for utility accounts and leadership alts.

🔗 Link Former Members

Someone who left and came back (maybe more than once) and was added as a new member? Pick them as the current member and tick their entries on the Former Members list. Their history (chests, troops, heroes, events, warnings, …) then counts for the current member, their old login comes along if they don't have one, and each earlier membership is kept on their profile. Entries under another name are renamed to the current member's name.

🔁 Member Transfers

Players who move between the cCc clans: ask the clan they came from for their profile (memberships, troop and hero levels, chest points, events, warnings), and answer the other clans' requests. Nothing is shared until a superior of that clan approves.

Linked Google Accounts

Link a member to a Google account so they can sign in and see their own bank balance and point penalties. The same address can be linked to several members — handy for alt accounts — and the profile page then offers a dropdown to switch between them. Members only get the public pages plus their profile; management pages are for superiors only (the Superiors list).

Superiors

Superiors can use the management pages (Events, Members, Warnings, Bank, Profiling, …) and make changes. Tick a member to make them a superior: the Google account(s) linked to them on Member Login Accounts get the rights, and lose them when the member leaves the clan. Logins that aren't clan members go under Extra e-mails. Only you can see this list; you are always a superior yourself.

Extra e-mails

Google logins with superior rights that aren't linked to a clan member. The note is just for you (e.g. who it is).
